# Roadmap

## Before a public launch

- Independently recheck all initial institutional claims and conflicting aid-form instructions.
- Verify Fall 2027 admission plans, deadlines, application platforms, fees/waivers and complete cost breakdowns with field-level sources.
- Add a broader range of institutions, including less selective and genuinely lower-cost options; avoid presenting eight highly selective schools as a balanced list.
- Conduct keyboard, screen-reader and contrast audits with students, plus Firefox/Safari and real mobile testing.
- Verify the intended public domain, social metadata and Vercel deployment configuration with the owner.

## Next useful product increments

- Verified competitive scholarships, including citizenship, subject and renewal constraints.
- European examples with country-specific currencies and program admissions requirements.
- A deliberate evidence-review schedule and editorial tooling for stale/conflicting records.
- Rule-based citizenship, test and deadline checks when sufficient data exists.
- Calendar export and opt-in reminders; clear separation of official and personal dates.
- Complete English/Russian dictionaries and localized route content, introduced together.
- Optional accounts and encrypted sync with explicit consent, migration and concurrency design.

These are future work, not working controls in the MVP.
