# AidMap core MVP upgrade

Prepared 23 October 2026. Target: international first-year applicants entering in Fall 2027. The existing repository, university IDs, 30-institution catalogue and hosting identity are retained. The subsequent owner-authorized public Preview release includes this upgrade; see [the October 24 release record](VERCEL_PREVIEW.md).

## Implemented workflows

1. **Sourced requirements:** per-round admission, financial-aid and enrollment checklists; student/school/counselor/teacher/parent ownership; required, recommended, optional and conditional distinctions; cross-shortlist matrix; reusable preparation separate from university-specific submission. Refreshing research is idempotent and preserves progress, notes, retired requirement keys and personal dates. “Prepared” does not mean sent. Not applicable requires a conditional item and a recorded explanation.
2. **Deadline planning:** category and upcoming/past/unresolved views, separate personal targets, user-entered triggers for relative deadlines, and selected-date ICS export. Bowdoin's non-Canadian aid deadline stays unresolved until an offer date is entered. Recurring dates without a confirmed year are not assigned a year or exported as fixed deadlines. No live calendar sync or notifications are promised.
3. **Explained research matches:** existing optional preferences feed separate subject, destination, funding, historical-score and language-condition explanations. MIT appears under Computer Science. No overall admissions score, odds or invented GPA conversion. School-specific English instruction durations remain distinct.
4. **Funding comparison:** compare two to four saved annual scenarios with year, provenance, currency, confirmed grant, hypothetical total award, contribution, remainder and gap. Assumptions can be restored and updated without duplicating the saved record. Currency changes clear amounts. Different currencies remain unconverted and unranked. Restrictions remain visible; an arithmetic remainder is not an award or institutional need assessment.
5. **Research navigation:** profile summaries, source disclosure, selectable university-comparison sections, normalized differences-only comparison, and a browser-print shortlist brief. Private notes are opt-in; scores and budgets are not included in the brief.

## Audit fixes

- Blank contribution remains unknown; zero remains zero. Negative contribution does not erase a valid expense total or confirmed-award remainder. Invalid hypothetical awards do not erase confirmed calculations. The 60,000 cost / 45,000 award case produces a 15,000 remainder in the correct separate scenario.
- Shared count wording handles singular/plural in saved records, funding results and progress. Developer-facing research instructions were removed from applicant copy.
- Funding filters survive reload through non-sensitive URL parameters. University discovery includes an explicit unknown-cost option; enabling it does not admit unrelated unknown requirements or known mismatches. Gross cost is identified as before aid.
- Admission date filtering uses event category, excluding aid and supporting-document deadlines. Undated recurring guidance is not represented as a verified Fall 2027 deadline.
- Search, theme and other key controls wait for client readiness; select controls have precise accessible names.
- Existing sourced core policies now feed search, cards, comparison and funding, not just the detailed profile. Official program names are retained alongside canonical search categories.

## Data, migration and privacy

`types/admissions.ts` validates source references, research status, applicable cycles, stable requirement/deadline keys, conditional explanations and round membership. `lib/core-catalogue.ts` adapts the new records into the retained catalogue without replacing other universities. There are 8 dossiers, 180 requirement records, 77 dated/recurring/relative events, and 500 catalogue field-evidence records.

The browser key remains `aidmap:v1`; the validated payload migrates version 1 to version 2. Existing IDs, notes, tasks, dates, preferences, shortlist, comparison, saved funding and scenarios survive. Added fields are optional. JSON import validates and previews before explicit replacement; invalid/incompatible data never replaces the workspace. CSV escapes quotes/newlines and neutralizes formula-leading text. ICS uses stable IDs, CRLF, escaped text and UTF-8-aware folding; all-day events end the following day, and known IANA cutoff times convert to UTC.

There is no applicant backend, analytics integration or new network submission. Profile, notes, budgets and scores remain browser-local and are not placed in discovery URLs. User text is rendered through React text nodes. JSON backups intentionally contain the user's workspace and are downloaded only on request. CSV includes selected shortlist data; the print brief excludes private notes unless explicitly opted in. Repository upload rules exclude local backups, credentials, screenshots, test reports, cache and deployment metadata.

## Official-source gaps and conflicts

- **Harvard:** latest successfully retrieved CDS is 2024–25; current recurring admission dates lack an explicit target year. Test-version/validity details were not verified.
- **Yale:** the $80 application fee is a CDS 2025–26 reference, not independently confirmed on the redesigned current checklist. Dates are recurring; aid requirements are personalized. Some English-version and final-report details remain unresolved.
- **Princeton:** detailed aid instructions request 2025 income while a timeline still names 2024. C7 table columns could not be verified visually. Numeric English thresholds, some test-version conditions, final-report timing and the health-plan price remain unverified.
- **MIT:** public essay guidance gives approximate ranges; exact limits are in the application. Recurring dates lack an explicit Fall 2027 year/timezone. CDS score-receipt guidance differs from current test-taking instructions; the current first-year instructions control. Insurance and some test-version details remain unresolved.
- **Amherst:** C9 cohort wording conflicts with C1. Formal C7 factor columns remain unverified. Some translation/prediction/final-report, ED signatory, English retake/validity, merit-policy and detailed renewal rules require confirmation. The explicit Fall 2027 calendar currently states November 9, 2026 for ED; this unusual date was rechecked on the official calendar rather than normalized to November 1.
- **Dartmouth:** the official application page uses both January 1 and January 2 for RD, so no definitive RD calendar date is invented. Travel wording does not reconcile with the published cost-component sum. Some translation, ED and English-version details remain unresolved.
- **Bowdoin:** admission dates are recurring; Canadian aid deadlines require route-specific confirmation. The seven-day non-Canadian post-offer rule is represented as relative. Prior-college eligibility details, originals with translated documents, English-version rules and insurance price need confirmation.
- **Brown:** the latest CDS dashboard could not be extracted; the official admitted-student statistics are used as a clearly labeled fallback. The general checklist lists four essays while special-program paragraphs still refer to three. Applicant-local cutoff wording lacks an IANA timezone; some translation, enrollment/testing verification and insurance details remain unresolved.
- **All eight:** 2027–28 cost quotes are not established by the 2026–27 baselines. Personalized aid offers and portals are outside this browser-local tool. The UI lists the complete per-institution gap record with its sources.
