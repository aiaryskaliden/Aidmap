# 100-university catalogue update

The work began at GitHub main commit `6e032a1`, with 70 distinct university profiles. This update adds 30 institutions while retaining all 70 original names and slugs. The validator checks the normalized university name and institutional domain as well as the slug. Campus choices are recorded in the programme notes when an institution offers the degree at more than one location.

The update contains **100 undergraduate profiles** across **27 countries and territories**, **89 funding options** and **1,105 source-linked evidence records**. Of the 30 additions, 13 have a dated Fall 2027 admission deadline, 29 have sourced document guidance, 19 have a published IELTS score with its scope, and 9 have a dated amount for annual tuition. Other intake dates, undated tuition tariffs, fee ranges and award conditions remain visible on their own terms.

## Where applicants see the evidence

Explore counts and filters the same catalogue that generates the profile routes. A profile’s **Overview** links the degree, **Admissions** shows qualifications, tests, documents and reporting instructions, and **Application and funding deadlines** displays the original intake. **Annual cost of attendance** displays official tuition terms alongside any dated estimate. **Scholarships** shows applicant conditions, coverage, separate application and renewal. **Sources** lists checked links and items that still need confirmation.

Only published dates are stored as calendar deadlines. Recurring month-and-day guidance, pending cycles and other intakes stay labeled with their own scope. Tuition-only coverage, demonstrated need and full attendance coverage remain separate. Published allowances have their own terms.
