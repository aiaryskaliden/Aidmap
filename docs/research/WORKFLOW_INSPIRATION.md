# Workflow inspiration and evidence method

Official product/help documentation was reviewed; no competitor account or hands-on application workflow was tested. AidMap's features below are design adaptations, not assertions that each service has an identical feature. No competitor branding, screenshots, layouts or proprietary datasets were copied.

| Official documentation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Observed principle                                                           | AidMap adaptation                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [BigFuture College Search](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/college-search/filters), [College Board score context guide](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/setting-target-score), [saved-college-list policy](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/program-specific-privacy-policies/bigfuture/college-lists) | Subject/location discovery, saved college lists and published score context  | Transparent filters, retained shortlist, historical submitter ranges with cohort labels; no probability estimate           |
| [Scoir My Colleges](https://help.scoir.com/article/eo07vpqsvt-my-colleges), [college-list table view](https://help.scoir.com/article/43ghnlfaf5-for-students-view-your-college-list-in-a-data-table-format)                                                                                                          | College-specific planning stages and compact list comparison                 | Per-university requirements, responsible person, admissions/aid stages and a shortlist matrix; self-reported progress only |
| [Appily College Match Quiz](https://www.appily.com/tools-quizzes/find-the-perfect-college)                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | A short preference-led discovery flow considering subject, location and cost | Existing optional start flow and preferences reused, with separate match explanations and unresolved affordability         |

BigFuture's JavaScript search application was not fully rendered by the research fetch; accessible official search/help descriptions support the limited observations above. Scoir and Appily descriptions were read as documentation, not proof that a real document was transmitted or an applicant result predicted.

## Institutional research method

- Use university admissions, financial-aid, academic-directory and institutional-research sources. Every stored fact references an HTTPS official source with checked date, cycle, applicant scope and research status.
- Keep admission cycle, score cohort and academic cost year independent. Recurring month/day guidance is not a confirmed year. Distinguish minimum, recommended and typical scores.
- Read CDS C7 table layout or form fields before assigning categories; extracted text alone may lose the factor-column relationship. Where reliable visual verification was unavailable, retain qualitative official guidance and an explicit gap.
- Do not add SAT section percentiles to invent a composite distribution. Preserve score-submitter and admitted/enrolled population differences.
- Keep inaccessible, unpublished, historical and conflicting facts distinct. An inaccessible official page is not evidence that the information is unpublished.
- The three core JSON modules contain normalized summaries and citations, not wholesale copies of official webpages. Transient downloaded source material and rendered research pages live in ignored `tmp/` and are excluded from deployment.

The schema and validation script enforce internal consistency; they do not prove that universities will retain their policies. Recheck the linked official sources and personalized portals before an application or payment.
