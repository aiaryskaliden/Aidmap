The starting catalogue has 34 distinct institution IDs, 10 countries, 41 funding entries and 593 field sources. The public Preview used for comparison is [AidMap Explore](https://aidmap-aiaryskaliden.vercel.app/explore). Selection starts with international need-based policies, then merit routes at public universities and additional CS programmes outside the United States. Admission and financial fit depend on the applicant. Listing a candidate does not verify an award.
