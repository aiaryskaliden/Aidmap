# AidMap catalogue expansion to 150

The catalogue adds 22 UK universities, 10 Canadian universities, 10 Australian universities, four Dutch universities, two Singaporean universities and two Hong Kong universities.

The UK research comes from the repository's [November 27 research branch](https://github.com/aiaryskaliden/Aidmap/tree/1fe9ce6332dfac3028151f5d36f86cb4a82c1f6a). Edinburgh, Manchester and Bristol were already in Explore and retain their original profiles and slugs. The other 28 profiles use individual programme, international-entry and fee pages. The link titles below lead to the programme evidence stored alongside each profile.

Tuition is displayed only for a published cycle. International pathways and fees vary by qualification, campus, load and year. Check each profile's source links for conditions. Unconfirmed deadlines, scores, rates, costs and aid terms are left unknown.
